## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, civic-editorial aesthetic engineered for high-density narrative storytelling, journalistic data transparency, and institutional authority. Designed for civic analytics and participatory narratives, it bridges the institutional weight of democratic processes with the clarity, accessibility, and dynamism of modern digital journalism.

### Aesthetic Movement & Tone
- **Civic Editorial Minimalism:** Driven by disciplined typographic hierarchy, crisp structural borders, calibrated negative space, and document-grade paper backgrounds reminiscent of high-end investigative journalism.
- **Data-First Rigor:** UI patterns prioritize legibility of metrics, categorical clarity across narratives, and visual integrity in charts, comparative indicators, and geo-data representations.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Impartial, credible, lucid, yet deeply human and civic. The interface feels trustworthy, official, and intellectually engaging without becoming cold or bureaucratic.

## Colors

The palette establishes an institutional yet vibrant civic identity built on high-contrast readability and strict communicative roles.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Primary (`#6B114D`) & INE Magenta (`#B3195B`):** Serves as the primary institutional anchor. Applied to dominant navigational landmarks, key story triggers, hero branding, and institutional milestones.
- **Secondary Civic Navy (`#1E3A8A`) & Civic Blue (`#3B82F6`):** Anchors analytical depth, categorical headers, and structured quantitative metrics.
- **Tertiary Civic Emerald (`#0D9488`):** Emphasizes active citizen participation, success indicators, interactive callouts, and data points demonstrating engagement or positive variance.
- **Paper Neutral (`#F9FAFB` & `#111827`):** The foundation replicates premium editorial stock (`#F9FAFB`) with deep ink neutrality (`#111827`), maintaining WCAG AAA compliance for running narrative copy.
- **Card and Layer Foundations:** Card surfaces use pristine `#FFFFFF` layered over the `#F9FAFB` backdrop, delimited by calibrated micro-borders (`#E5E7EB`) to retain crisp boundaries under daylight conditions.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a tension between the welcoming, high-impact geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and metrics, and the utilitarian precision of **Inter** for sustained reading and dense data displays.

### Typographic Rules
- **Numerical Dominance:** Use `stat-counter` exclusively for large-format KPIs, percentages, and demographic metrics. Always pair metrics with an uppercase `label-sm` subtitle for civic terminology.
- **Editorial Flow:** Long-form civic case studies must maintain an optimal reading measure between 60 to 75 characters per line using `body-lg` or `body-md` on `#F9FAFB` paper backgrounds.
- **Metric Micro-data:** For data tables, map tooltips, and footnote attributions, use tabular lining numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in `body-sm` and `caption`.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a 12-column responsive grid framed by deliberate journalistic whitespace to maintain composure across complex visual narratives.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`. Enables side-by-side narrative text paired with dynamic charts, interactive maps, or pull-quotes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Visualizations stack beneath lead story summaries or take full column width.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Single-column story reflow with persistent top metric bars and bottom drawer controls for filters.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Spacing follows an 8pt increment model.
- Internal component padding uses `space-md` (compact widgets) and `space-lg` (standard story cards).
- Distinct narrative sections use `space-2xl` vertical separation to emulate broadsheet chapter breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp architectural surface tiers rather than aggressive drop shadows, preserving an authentic editorial feel.

### Depth Stratification
- **Ground (Level 0):** Document canvas `#F9FAFB`. Flat, non-reflective base.
- **Editorial Cards (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with a sharp 1px border in `#E5E7EB` and an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive / Hover Surface (Level 2):** Applied when cards or map tiles receive focus or hover: border transitions to `#CBD5E1` with an elevated ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals (Level 3):** Dropdown filters, tooltip callouts, and story detail drawers: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`.
- **Backdrop Scrim:** Modal overlays use `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.4)` accompanied by a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness model (`roundedness: 2`). This keeps containers structural, clean, and serious, while softening touch points and category tags.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Standard Cards, Data Panes, & Modals:** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Clean, rectangular stability that reinforces the editorial layout.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, & Form Selects:** `6px` to `8px` (`0.375rem` to `0.5rem`). Crisp and clearly defined.
- **Chips, Category Tags, & Badges:** `9999px` (Pill format). Creates instant distinction between interactive/metadata tags and structural content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Background `#6B114D`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`, height `44px`. Hover state shifts to `#B3195B` with a gentle transition (`150ms ease-in-out`). Active state: scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary Data Button:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1E3A8A`. Hover state adds surface tint `#F8FAFC` and border `#1E3A8A`.
- **Civic Action Button:** Background `#0D9488`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for participatory calls to action, survey submissions, and filter triggers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Story Category Chips:** Pill shape, height `28px`, padding `0 12px`. Light tone fill (`#CCFBF1` with `#0D9488` text for Civic topics; `#FCE7F3` with `#B3195B` text for INE highlights; `#DBEAFE` with `#1E3A8A` text for Data/Demographics).
- **Metric Trend Badges:** Inline indicators with a 4px icon arrow and tabular percentage, utilizing semitransparent green/red contextual fills.

### Cards & Story Containers
- **Narrative Story Card:** Base `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, padding `space-lg`, border-radius `0.5rem`. Top hairline border (3px) colored according to the narrative theme (Magenta, Navy, or Emerald) to establish immediate story attribution.
- **Metric / KPI Tile:** Minimal padding (`space-md`), prominent `stat-counter` number in `#111827`, flanked by secondary subtitle labels and sparkline previews.

### Data Tables & Interactive Lists
- Rows have fixed minimum heights of `52px` with horizontal divider borders (`#E5E7EB`).
- Alternating hover background `#F9FAFB`. Column headers styled with `label-sm`, uppercase, `#4B5563`, with sort indicators.

### Form Inputs, Search & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #D1D5DB`, text `#111827`, height `42px`. Focused state: border color `#6B114D`, with an exterior focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(107, 17, 77, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` geometric controls. Checked state: `#6B114D` fill with white indicator mark.

### Narrative Progress & Breadcrumbs
- Thin 2px progress bar fixed to the viewport top in `#B3195B` tracking reading progress across the six participatory stories.
